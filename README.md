# Personalised Beauty Product Recommendation

## Project Overview

This project is about using data science to explore personalised beauty product recommendations based on customer purchasing behaviour.

The aim is to understand customer preferences and purchasing patterns and explore how this information can be used to recommend beauty products that may be relevant to individual customers.

## Project Area

* Beauty and Wellness
* Data Science
* Customer Behaviour Analysis
* Product Recommendation
* Personalised Recommendations

## Week 1 – Problem Definition

In Week 1, I studied the beauty and wellness industry and identified a business problem related to choosing suitable beauty products from a large number of available products.

I also studied current industry trends, customer requirements, possible data sources, important variables, research questions, and possible data science approaches.

## Project Objective

The main objective is to explore whether customer purchasing behaviour and product information can be analysed to identify patterns and provide personalised beauty product recommendations.

## Planned Project Approach

The project will be developed in stages:

1. Problem definition
2. Data collection
3. Data cleaning
4. Exploratory data analysis
5. Feature engineering
6. Recommendation approach
7. Evaluation and business recommendations
